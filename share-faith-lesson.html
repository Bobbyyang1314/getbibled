Learn to Share Your Faith

A Scripture-centered introduction to telling your story, explaining the Gospel, responding with love, and helping new believers follow Christ.

Introductory teaching draft for review. The expanded manual will be drawn from approved ministry materials.

Why we share

Jesus sends His followers to bear witness to Him. You do not need to know every answer to share the truth about the Savior. Be faithful, compassionate, and clear.

Acts 1:8 · KJV

But ye shall receive power, after that the Holy Ghost is come upon you: and ye shall be witnesses unto me both in Jerusalem, and in all Judaea, and in Samaria, and unto the uttermost part of the earth.

Begin with prayer and listening

Pray for an open door. Listen to the person. You might ask, “May I tell you what Jesus Christ has done in my life?” Speak with respect and be willing to hear their questions. Do not let fear of offense cause you to omit the Gospel.

Colossians 4:6 · KJV

Let your speech be alway with grace, seasoned with salt, that ye may know how ye ought to answer every man.

Share your testimony in three parts

First: What was your life like before you followed Jesus? Second: How did you recognize your need for Him and turn to Him? Third: How has He changed your life? Keep your testimony truthful, concise, and focused on Christ rather than on your past.

Mark 5:19 · KJV

Howbeit Jesus suffered him not, but saith unto him, Go home to thy friends, and tell them how great things the Lord hath done for thee, and hath had compassion on thee.

Explain the whole Gospel

Explain who God is and why sin separates us from Him. Tell the good news that Jesus Christ died for our sins, was buried, and rose again. We cannot earn salvation by good works. The Gospel is more than a promise of comfort; it is the announcement that Jesus saves sinners.

Romans 5:8 · KJV

But God commendeth his love toward us, in that, while we were yet sinners, Christ died for us.

Call for repentance and faith

Jesus calls us to repent and believe the Gospel. Repentance is turning from sin toward God; faith is trusting Jesus Christ as Savior and Lord. Invite a sincere response without pressuring someone to repeat a formula. Christ saves, not the words of a prayer.

Mark 1:15 · KJV

And saying, The time is fulfilled, and the kingdom of God is at hand: repent ye, and believe the gospel.

Answer questions graciously

People may ask about suffering, hypocrisy, forgiveness, or whether Jesus is the only way. Listen carefully. If you do not know an answer, say so and offer to study Scripture together. Never replace the Gospel with an argument.

2 Timothy 2:24 · KJV

And the servant of the Lord must not strive; but be gentle unto all men, apt to teach, patient,

Follow up and make disciples

If someone turns to Christ, encourage them to read Scripture, pray, seek baptism, and join a faithful local church. Help them take the next step with What’s Next? and the four-part Way of a Disciple course.

Matthew 28:20 · KJV

Teaching them to observe all things whatsoever I have commanded you: and, lo, I am with you alway, even unto the end of the world. Amen.

Practice what you learned

Write your testimony in three parts and practice telling it in two minutes. Then practice explaining the Gospel clearly to another believer. Pray for one person with whom you can share Jesus this week.
