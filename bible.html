Explore a Word in Hebrew or Greek

In the finished reader, selecting a word will open its original-language entry with Hebrew or Greek spelling, transliteration, Strong’s number, grammatical information where available, contextual meaning, and other occurrences. The prototype links to an external study tool until appropriately licensed data are integrated.

Example word study (Greek): John 3:16, “loved” — ἀγαπάω (agapaō), Strong’s G25. The word’s meaning must be interpreted in the context of the verse, not merely from a list of possible definitions.
