FOUNDATIONAL BIBLE LESSON · APPROXIMATELY 5–10 MINUTES

How Can I Be Saved?

Understand the Gospel of Jesus Christ, why you need salvation, and how to respond through repentance and faith.

This is a full introductory teaching draft for your review. The final edition will be reconciled with your approved manual before publication.

Why do I need to be saved?

Many people try to live decent lives. Yet the Bible says our greatest problem is deeper than bad habits or difficult circumstances. We have sinned against God—in what we do, say, think, and desire. None of us meets God's perfect standard.

Sin separates us from the God who made us. We cannot erase guilt by comparing ourselves with someone else, joining a church, or doing enough good deeds. We need God's forgiveness and a new heart.

Romans 3:23 · KJV

For all have sinned, and come short of the glory of God;

Romans 6:23 · KJV

For the wages of sin is death; but the gift of God is eternal life through Jesus Christ our Lord.

God is holy, but He loves us

God is righteous and must judge evil. He is also full of love and mercy. The Gospel does not ask us to pretend that sin is harmless. It announces that God Himself has provided the Savior we could never provide for ourselves.

You do not need to clean up your entire life before coming to Christ. You do need to come honestly, acknowledging your sin and your need for His mercy.

Romans 5:8 · KJV

But God commendeth his love toward us, in that, while we were yet sinners, Christ died for us.

Who is Jesus, and what did He do?

Jesus Christ is the Son of God. He came into the world, lived without sin, taught the truth, healed the afflicted, and willingly gave His life on the cross. He bore the penalty of sin, was buried, and rose again on the third day.

His resurrection means that Jesus is not merely a teacher whose ideas we admire. He is the living Lord. The Gospel is not simply advice about becoming a better person; it is the good news of what God has done in Jesus Christ.

1 Corinthians 15:3–4 · KJV

For I delivered unto you first of all that which I also received, how that Christ died for our sins according to the scriptures; And that he was buried, and that he rose again the third day according to the scriptures:

John 14:6 · KJV

Jesus saith unto him, I am the way, the truth, and the life: no man cometh unto the Father, but by me.

Repent: turn from sin toward God

Jesus did not preach faith without repentance. To repent is to change your mind and turn your heart toward God, agreeing with Him about sin and turning away from it. Repentance is more than regret about consequences; it is a sincere response to God's truth.

Repentance does not mean you will never struggle again. It means you no longer want to defend your sin or rule your own life apart from Christ. You turn to Him for forgiveness and a new beginning.

Mark 1:15 · KJV

And saying, The time is fulfilled, and the kingdom of God is at hand: repent ye, and believe the gospel.

Acts 3:19 · KJV

Repent ye therefore, and be converted, that your sins may be blotted out, when the times of refreshing shall come from the presence of the Lord;

Believe the Gospel and receive Jesus Christ

Saving faith is not merely agreeing that Jesus existed. It is trusting in Him—His death, His resurrection, and His promise of salvation. You cannot earn eternal life. You receive God's gift by grace through faith.

Confess Jesus as Lord. Believe in your heart that God raised Him from the dead. Call on Him sincerely. The Bible promises that those who receive Christ become children of God.

Romans 10:9 · KJV

That if thou shalt confess with thy mouth the Lord Jesus, and shalt believe in thine heart that God hath raised him from the dead, thou shalt be saved.

John 1:12 · KJV

But as many as received him, to them gave he power to become the sons of God, even to them that believe on his name:

Ephesians 2:8–9 · KJV

For by grace are ye saved through faith; and that not of yourselves: it is the gift of God: Not of works, lest any man should boast.

How can I respond to Jesus today?

You can respond to God now. Acknowledge your sin. Turn from it toward Him. Tell Jesus you believe He died for your sins and rose again. Ask Him to forgive you, save you, and lead your life. You may pray in your own words; there are no magic words that save anyone.

For example, you might say: “Lord Jesus, I know I have sinned and need Your mercy. I believe You died for my sins and rose again. I turn from my sin and put my trust in You. Forgive me, save me, and teach me to follow You. Amen.” This prayer expresses repentance and faith; it is Christ who saves, not the recitation of a prayer.

Jesus calls us not only to begin, but to follow Him. A genuine new life grows in obedience, love, and faithfulness. If you have turned to Christ, the next lesson explains your first steps.

Romans 10:13 · KJV

For whosoever shall call upon the name of the Lord shall be saved.

Luke 9:23 · KJV

And he said to them all, If any man will come after me, let him deny himself, and take up his cross daily, and follow me.
