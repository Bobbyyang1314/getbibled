NEW BELIEVER LESSON · APPROXIMATELY 5–10 MINUTES

I Have Received Jesus. What’s Next?

Seven simple, biblical steps to help you begin walking with Christ.

Introductory teaching draft for editorial review. The full What’s Next? booklet will be added once the approved file is supplied.

1. Know that you belong to Him

If you have repented and trusted Jesus Christ, God’s promise stands, not your feelings. Your assurance rests on what Christ has done.

1 John 5:13 · KJV

These things have I written unto you that believe on the name of the Son of God; that ye may know that ye have eternal life, and that ye may believe on the name of the Son of God.

2. Talk with God every day

Prayer is simply speaking honestly with your Father. Set aside a quiet time each day, thank Him, confess your sins, and ask for what you need.

Matthew 6:6 · KJV

But thou, when thou prayest, enter into thy closet, and when thou hast shut thy door, pray to thy Father which is in secret; and thy Father which seeth in secret shall reward thee openly.

3. Read the Bible

Begin with the Gospel of John, then continue through the New Testament. Read a little each day, ask what it teaches about God and about how to live, and do what it says.

1 Peter 2:2 · KJV

As newborn babes, desire the sincere milk of the word, that ye may grow thereby:

4. Turn from sin and keep following

You will still struggle at times. When you fall, do not hide. Confess, receive forgiveness, and keep going.

1 John 1:9 · KJV

If we confess our sins, he is faithful and just to forgive us our sins, and to cleanse us from all unrighteousness.

5. Be baptized

Jesus commanded His followers to be baptized. Baptism does not earn salvation; it publicly shows that you belong to Christ. Speak with a pastor about your next step.

Romans 6:4 · KJV

Therefore we are buried with him by baptism into death: that like as Christ was raised up from the dead by the glory of the Father, even so we also should walk in newness of life.

6. Join a faithful local church

You were not meant to follow Jesus alone. Find a church that teaches the Bible and loves people, and meet regularly with other believers.

Hebrews 10:25 · KJV

Not forsaking the assembling of ourselves together, as the manner of some is; but exhorting one another: and so much the more, as ye see the day approaching.

7. Tell others

Share what Jesus has done for you with family and friends. You do not need to know everything to say what you have seen and experienced.

Mark 5:19 · KJV

Howbeit Jesus suffered him not, but saith unto him, Go home to thy friends, and tell them how great things the Lord hath done for thee, and hath had compassion on thee.
