FOUNDATIONAL BIBLE LESSON · APPROXIMATELY 5–10 MINUTES

Who Is God?

Discover who God is, what He is like, and how He has made Himself known in Jesus Christ.

Introductory teaching draft for editorial review. It will be reconciled with the approved manual before publication.

God is the Creator

The Bible begins with God, not with an argument for His existence. He is the eternal Creator who made everything that exists, and He is distinct from His creation.

Genesis 1:1 · KJV

In the beginning God created the heaven and the earth.

God is holy

God is perfectly pure, and there is no sin in Him. His holiness is the reason sin matters, and it is also the reason that salvation is so wonderful: the Holy One has made a way for sinners to come to Him.

Isaiah 6:3 · KJV

And one cried unto another, and said, Holy, holy, holy, is the LORD of hosts: the whole earth is full of his glory.

God is love

God’s love is not only a feeling. It moved Him to give His Son for us. Holiness and love meet at the cross of Jesus Christ.

1 John 4:8 · KJV

He that loveth not knoweth not God; for God is love.

One God: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit

Scripture teaches that there is one God, who exists eternally as Father, Son, and Holy Spirit. The Son became a man in Jesus Christ. The Holy Spirit dwells in and empowers believers. This is a mystery that we receive because God has revealed it.

Matthew 28:19 · KJV

Go ye therefore, and teach all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Ghost:

God has made Himself known in Jesus

We cannot know God fully by our own effort. He came to us in Jesus Christ, who shows us what God is like.

John 1:14 · KJV

And the Word was made flesh, and dwelt among us, (and we beheld his glory, the glory as of the only begotten of the Father,) full of grace and truth.

Can I know Him?

Yes. God invites us to know Him personally through Jesus Christ. Read how you can be forgiven and begin a new life with Him.
